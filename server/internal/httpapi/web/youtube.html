Storage policy

One download or conversion at a time. New work requires the reserve plus 24 GB of workspace. Space is checked during downloads and conversion. Compact mode uses two CPU threads; the original is kept unless a smaller copy passes video/audio/duration checks. Changing policy interrupts active work so it can restart under the new settings.
